About us

A small bakery with a big celebration spirit

Buhle's Bakery began in 2022 as a home-based bakery serving cakes and cupcakes to the local community. It has grown into a trusted neighbourhood business known for quality, affordability, and beautiful cake designs.

Our Story

What started as a passion for baking at home has grown into a small business serving customers throughout Kroonstad. Every cake is made using fresh ingredients, attention to detail, and a commitment to making every celebration memorable.

We enjoy working closely with customers to create personalised cakes that reflect their preferred colours, themes, flavours, and unique ideas for every occasion.

Our Mission

To provide fresh, high-quality baked goods made with love and passion while delivering excellent customer service to every customer.

Our Vision

To become a leading bakery brand known for delicious products, creative designs, and strong community connections.
