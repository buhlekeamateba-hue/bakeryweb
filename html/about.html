About Us

Buhle’s Bakery is a fictional small business established in 2022. It began as a home-based bakery providing freshly baked cakes and cupcakes to the local community. Due to increasing demand and customer loyalty, the business has grown a lot into a well-recognized neighbourhood bakery known for quality and affordability.

Mission

To provide fresh, high quality baked goods made with love and passion, while delivering excellent customer service.

Vision

To become a leading bakery brand known for delicious products, innovation, and community connection.
